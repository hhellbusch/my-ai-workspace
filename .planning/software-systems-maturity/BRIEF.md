**Audience:** Workspace owner and future agents continuing maturity work.

**Purpose:** Resurrect a 2016–2017 maturity deck as a **trailhead + deep dives**, then integrate it as an organizing lens for this repository — without turning the workspace into a certification bureaucracy.
