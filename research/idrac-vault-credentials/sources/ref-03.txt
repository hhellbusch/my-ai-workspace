# Internal iDRAC credential management (OME 4.3.x User's Guide)

Enabling internal iDRAC credential management during initial onboarding allows OpenManage Enterprise to create a local service account on supported iDRACs after first successful discovery. Username format: `OME_[application-GUID]`. Retrieve application GUID via GET `/api/ApplicationService/Info`.

## Prerequisites
- OpenManage Enterprise Advanced or Advanced+ license (also supported with OpenManage Essential Server Configuration Management license on iDRAC).
- Requires a free local account slot (iDRAC max 16 local accounts). If exceeded, service account cannot be created; communication falls back to discovery credentials.
- Password Strength Policy must NOT be configured on iDRACs. If configured and OME-generated password fails policy, iDRAC disables the service account.

## Steps
Select Enable Internal iDRAC credential management during initial onboarding. OME creates the local service account after first successful discovery.

## Results / caveats
- No opt-out once enabled.
- If service account disabled by password strength policy, customers cannot manage those iDRACs from OME.
- No user roles assigned to this service account.
